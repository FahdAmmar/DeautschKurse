📖 Grammar & Article Guide

der — Maskulinum (Masculine)

Used for masculine nouns. The accusative form changes to den.

Ich kaufe den Tisch. (I buy the table.)

die — Femininum (Feminine)

Used for feminine nouns and ALL plural forms. The plural article is always die.

Ich setze mich auf die Couch. (I sit on the couch.)

das — Neutrum (Neuter)

Used for neuter nouns. Often used for diminutives ending in -chen or -lein.

Das Regal ist voll. (The shelf is full.)

💡 Common Mistake

German articles must match the grammatical gender, not the natural gender. Always learn a noun with its article!

Say: "der Stuhl" — not just "Stuhl"

📝 Exam Tips

Nouns ending in -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft are always die. Nouns ending in -er for male persons are der.

die Möbel (furniture) — always plural in German!

🧠 Memory Trick

Use color-coding: Blue = der, Pink = die, Gold = das. Try to picture the word in its color when memorizing.

der (🔵 blue) Schrank, die (🩷 pink) Lampe, das (🟡 gold) Bett
