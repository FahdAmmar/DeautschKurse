📖 Grammar & Article Guide

der — Maskulinum (Masculine)

Many male animals and most "-er" ending nouns are der. The accusative form changes to den.

Ich sehe den Hund. (I see the dog.)

die — Femininum (Feminine)

Female animal forms often use die. Nouns ending in -e, -ung, -heit are usually feminine.

Die Katze schläft. (The cat is sleeping.)

das — Neutrum (Neuter)

Young animals (Diminutives with -chen/-lein) and many loan words are das.

Das Pferd läuft schnell. (The horse runs fast.)

💡 Common Mistake

Animal gender often differs from biological sex! das Mädchen (the girl) is neuter. Always learn the article with the noun!

Say: "der Hund" — not just "Hund"

📝 Exam Tips

Female animal forms often add -in: der Löwe → die Löwin. Young animals often use das: das Küken (the chick), das Lamm (the lamb).

der Wolf → die Wölfin (female wolf)

🧠 Memory Trick

Use color-coding: Blue = der, Pink = die, Gold = das. Picture each animal wearing its color to memorize the article!

der (🔵) Hund, die (🩷) Katze, das (🟡) Pferd
